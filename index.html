Questions, answered.

Cannot find yours? Call us or send a message and we will help.

How do I book an appointment online?

Use the booking form at the top of this page. Choose a service, date and time, and leave your phone number. We confirm by SMS.

Can I see a doctor without coming in?

Yes. Choose Video consultation. You will get a secure link by SMS to join from your phone or computer.

How do I get my lab results?

Results are uploaded to your patient account as soon as they are ready, and we send you an SMS to let you know.

Which payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, cards, cash at the hospital, and most major insurance covers. Ask the billing desk about your scheme.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call our emergency line or come straight to casualty. It is open 24 hours a day, every day.
